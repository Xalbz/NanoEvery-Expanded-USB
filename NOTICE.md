# Notice and upstream credits

Nano Every Expanded USB is an unofficial community project by **BlaX / Xalbz**.

It builds on and modifies behavior originating from Arduino's Nano Every MuxTO firmware and Arduino SAMD/megaAVR core sources. Historical MattairTech SAMD11/BOSSA tooling and CMSIS-Atmel material were also used to reconstruct the build/recovery environment.

The project is intended to preserve upstream licensing and attribution for Arduino-derived material. Arduino and Nano are trademarks of their respective owners. This project is not endorsed by or affiliated with Arduino.
